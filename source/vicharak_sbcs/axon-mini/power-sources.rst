Power Sources
=============

Power Rating
------------

.. important::

   - **Minimum Power:** 10W (5V 2A or 12V 0.83A)
   - **Recommended Power:** 15W (5V @ 3A or 12V @ 1.25A)
   
   If you are connecting all peripherals, such as Cameras, Displays, USB, HDMI, PCIe etc. the power requirement will range between **20W-25W** (up to 5V @ 5A or 12V @ 2.1A). This is necessary when the board is running at full load.

The Axon Mini board offers multiple versatile power options to suit various use cases. Below are the 5 supported power sources:

1. 12V Power Source (Dedicated Type-C PD Port)
----------------------------------------------
You can power the board using a 12V adapter through the dedicated Type-C PD port.

- **Minimum Power:** 10W
- **Recommended Power:** 15W
- **Maximum Power:** 30W



2. 5V Power Source (Dedicated Type-C PD Port)
---------------------------------------------
The same dedicated Type-C PD port can also accept a 5V power source.



3. Type-C/DP Port Power
-----------------------
The standard Type-C/DP port can also be used to power the board with a standard PD adapter.


4. Power Over Ethernet (PoE)
----------------------------
Axon Mini supports Power over Ethernet (PoE), allowing you to deliver both data and power over a single Ethernet cable. This is highly useful for deployments where electrical outlets are scarce, such as remote camera installations, IoT gateways, and headless servers.



5. Battery Power
----------------
For portable or backup power applications, the board features a 3.7V battery connector.

==========================
Axon Mini Video Interface
==========================

Axon Mini, powered by the Qualcomm Dragonwing **QCS6490**, includes an Adreno
Video Processing Unit (VPU) for hardware-accelerated video encode and decode.
This section covers the video encode and decode capabilities of the platform.

#####################################
Axon Lite Quadruple USB 3.0 Hub HAT
#####################################

.. image:: /_static/images/rk3576-axon-lite/axon-lite-quad-usb.webp
   :width: 70%
   :align: center

.. note::

   Connect the Expansion board while Axon Lite is powered off to avoid any damage.

The Axon Lite Quadruple USB 3.0 Hub HAT is compatible with Axon Lite and can be easily installed to expand USB connectivity. It allows you to connect up to four additional USB 3.0 devices to your Axon Lite SBC, utilizing the PCIe port for high-speed data transfer.

Specifications:
----------------

- PCIe to 4x USB 3.0 Interface
- High-speed data transfer
- Uses Axon Lite PCIe Port

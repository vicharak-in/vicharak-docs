######################
Axon Lite SATA Adapter
######################

.. .. image:: ../../../_static/images/rk3576-axon-lite/axon-lite-hat-sata.webp
.. :width: 30%
.. :align: center

.. note::

   Connect Expansion board while Axon Lite is powered off to avoid any damage and SATA enumeration can only be done during bootup, it not hot-pluggable.

Axon Lite SATA Hat is compatible with Axon Lite and can be easily installed to enable SATA connectivity. It allows you to connect SATA hard drives or solid-state drives (SSDs) to your Axon Lite SBC, providing additional storage capacity for your projects.

Specifications:
----------------

- 16 Pin FPC Cable
- SATA 3.0 Interface
